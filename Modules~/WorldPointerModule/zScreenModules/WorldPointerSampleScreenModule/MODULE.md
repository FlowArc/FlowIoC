# WorldPointerSampleScreen

## Purpose
The display WorldPointer's test module draws its pointers on: a screen on Layer_3 with three
`SampleLabelLayer`s, one per off-screen mode, each showing a word on a tinted panel. It is the
shape a game's own pointer screen takes, at sample size.

## Concepts
world pointer sample, sample screen, display, WorldPointerLayer, SampleLabelLayer,
SampleLabelIndicator, RegisterDisplay

## Decisions
- **Editor-only.** Every script is under `#if UNITY_EDITOR`, the screen loads from Resources, and
  its prefab and the indicator prefab sit under the module's `Editor/` folder, so nothing of it
  reaches a player build or an Addressables group. It exists for the test module's scene.
- **Listed on `WorldPointerTestRoot` only.** The sample is not a feature of the WorldPointer
  service, so its context is not on `WorldPointerServiceRoot`, which ships.
- **Registering is a Command's.** The Mediator dispatches on `ShowCompleted` and `HideCompleted`,
  and `RegisterSampleDisplaysCommand` / `UnregisterSampleDisplaysCommand` call the service - a
  Mediator injects nothing but its View.
- The ids and the content VO sit in this module's Shared assembly because the sample has no
  module owning the targets; in a game they belong to the owner's Shared.
