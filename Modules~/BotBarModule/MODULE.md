# BotBar

## Purpose
The hub's bottom tab bar - Home, Battle, Clan, Collection, Shop - as a generic, install-once
System with its own screen. The tabs, their number, their look and every animation come from
`CD_BotBar`; a tab is announced by its config key through a signal created per key, so the game's
Connector wires "the tab keyed shop" to its Shop screen and the module never holds that name.

## Using it
1. `BotBarSystemRoot` into the hub scene under the `0 - 97` band (it is at `2`, with
   `BotBarConnectorRoot` at `3` as its child). `CD_BotBar` and `RD_BotBar` ship filed in its
   Shared Scriptables.
2. Edit `CD_BotBar`: one entry per tab - key, title, icons, locked label - the `StartTab`, and the
   options: scale, raise, widen, duration and curve, the highlight, titles, the NEW mark, the slide.
3. In your ConnectorModule, a `BotBarConnectorSubContext`: `Main.Outgoing.Started` to
   `Incoming.Open`; `Outgoing.Selected("shop")` to your Shop screen's open signal, one line per
   tab; your unlock announcement `(string, bool)` to `Incoming.SetLocked`; a count to
   `Incoming.SetBadge`; a popup's open and close to `Incoming.Hide` and `Show`;
   `Outgoing.SelectionChanged` to analytics through a converter.
4. The bar's screen sits on layer 2; pages share layer 0 and open with `ForceOpenAtFullLayer`.
   Override the layer on the Root's entry if your layout differs.

## Decisions
- **A signal per config key.** `Outgoing.Selected(key)` returns one `Signal` per key, created on
  the first ask and named by hand for the console. A Connector picks an edge by name and decides
  nothing; a single `TabSelected(key)` would have needed an `if` somewhere, and a fixed signal per
  tab cannot exist in a module that does not know its tabs. `Launch` warns about a key no tab
  carries.
- **No unlock policy.** The bar knows no levels; `SetLocked(key, locked)` is the game's decision
  arriving. A locked tab stays tappable and answers with `LockedTabTapped`, for the toast.
- **Open announces nothing outward.** The game's boot opens its home page itself; the bar only
  puts itself on `StartTab`.
- **Locking the selected tab moves the selection to `StartTab`**, through the same path a tap
  takes; if `StartTab` is locked too the selection stays and a warning says so.
- **A Connector sub module of its own.** The screen may not reference its parent's Signals
  assembly, so the System-to-screen crossing is `BotBarScreenConnectorSubContext`, shipped under
  `zSubModules` and listed on the child Root.
- **No tween library.** Coroutines over the config's curves.
- **Install once.** A bar is a starter a game rewrites - the prefab, the icons, the tab list.

## Known gaps
- The pages behind the tabs are the game's: which screen opens, sliding between pages, pooling.
- The Device Debugger's signal panel reads holders by their fields and does not list `Selected(key)`.
- No Android back handling, no sound or haptics (hang them off `SelectionChanged`), no persistence
  of the selection.
