# BotBarScreen

## Purpose
The bar on stage: as many `BotBarTabEntity`s as the runtime lists, the selected one scaled,
raised and widened by the options that are on, a highlight under it, badges, the locked and NEW
marks, and a slide off the bottom edge. It reports a tap and decides nothing.

## Concepts
bottom bar screen, tab entity, BotBarScreenView, BotBarTabEntity, BotBarTab prefab, slide, highlight

## Decisions
- **Filled from the assets, not from the signal.** The opening Command reads `CD_BotBar` and
  `RD_BotBar` through `ISharedDataModel`; a bar already up is fetched and refilled, because the
  whole state at Open is one event.
- **The show animation is the slide in**, the hide animation the slide out; `Show` and `Hide`
  slide without closing the screen.
- **Widen through `LayoutElement.flexibleWidth`** under a `HorizontalLayoutGroup`: the layout is
  what slides the neighbours.

Update: never
